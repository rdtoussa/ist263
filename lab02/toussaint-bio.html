Ryann Toussaint's Biography

Hi! My name is Ryann Toussaint, and I am currently a junior at Syracuse University studying Communication Design in the school of Visual and Performing Arts. With this, I am interested in everything surrounding the world of branding. Brand strategy, brand marketing, brand design, and more. I enjoy advertising as well and hope to better my skills by building media campaigns and market research for future endeavors.

With my design background I have been able to use my craft in many extracurricular activities such as working as a designer and creative director for publication organizations at Syracuse and being a part of an internship that have pushed my classroom skills into real world work.
